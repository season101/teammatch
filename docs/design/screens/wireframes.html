08

Discover deck

Purpose
One role on one project, one decision. The deck is ranked by skill overlap.
Primary action
Apply (✓, swipe right, →)
Keys on desktop
← Pass · → Apply · ↑ Details · ↓ Close · Z Undo pass · ␣ Next page · ⇧→ Apply with note

Default

Role page: meta line, Why you fit, Team so far with "+ you?" on this seat, and a one-line pitch. Role · Project · Team tabs sit under the body; tap the left or right half to page, Space on desktop. Undo stays grey until you pass.

Mid-drag with a stamp

Tilt is dx/20, capped at 15° (phone dx 140 → 7°, desktop dx −160 → −8°). Stamp opacity follows the drag; past 30% of card width it is full and release commits. Phone shows APPLY, desktop shows PASS.

Out of roles

No more open roles you haven't applied to or passed (UC-07 2a).

Benched

After a team locks, every member sees this instead of cards until the term ends (BR-08, BR-09). Owners too: they are members of their own team.
